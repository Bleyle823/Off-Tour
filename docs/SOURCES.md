# Sources

Facts used in the plan, with where they came from. Accessed 8 Oct 2026.

## Kenya aviation
- KCAA, Unmanned Aircraft Systems page: https://kcaa.or.ke/safety-security-oversight/unmanned-aircraft-systems
- KCAA UAS Manual of Implementing Standards (categories, risk assessment lists national parks and aerodromes): https://kcaa.or.ke/sites/default/files/docs/uas/Manual%20of%20Implementing%20Standards%20(MIS).pdf
- Civil Aviation (UAS) Regulations, 2020 (privacy and imaging rules, wildlife investigation use with approval): https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2020/42/eng@2020-04-03
- Civil Aviation (Remote Piloted Aircraft Systems) Regulations, 2017 (10 km and 7 km aerodrome rule, approach paths): https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2017/259/eng@2017-12-22
- AfriScan summary of the 2025 regulations (Legal Notice 40 of 2026), **secondary source, verify**: https://afri-scan.com/ke/drone-regulations
- Autonomy Global, BVLOS in Africa (WildDrone trial at Ol Pejeta, Zipline precedent): https://www.autonomyglobal.co/how-to-navigate-bvlos-regulations-in-africa/

- Park rules (drones need written KWS authorization): https://nairobipark.org/nairobi-national-park-rules/
- KWS conservation fee schedule, October 2025 (drone charges): https://www.kws.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-12/KWS%20Conservation%20Fee%20October%202025.pdf
- KWS park page: https://www.kws.go.ke/nairobi-national-park
- Draft management plan 2020-2030 (117 km2, fenced on three sides, aircraft noise, species): https://conservationalliance.or.ke/publications/3-nairobi-national-park-draft-management-plan/file

- Wilson Airport flight-path discussion (Daily Nation): https://nation.africa/kenya/news/questions-over-state-plan-to-alter-wilson-airport-flight-path-5510700

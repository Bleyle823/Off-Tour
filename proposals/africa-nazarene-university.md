**OFF TOUR** is a careful way for people who cannot travel to see a Kenyan park, and an extra pair of eyes for rangers on approved roads. In simulation, an autonomous Clearpath Moose stays on designated tourist self-drive paths and keeps distance from animals. It is trained on that circuit and drives by itself; there is no driver aboard. A classroom or remote visitor turns the camera only. Rhinos and other sensitive species stay off any live public feed; locations are not published. The product is for Kenya’s parks in general, with a practical first step at **Nairobi National Park**; other parks only with written permission. Aircraft are not part of the product.

Kenya’s parks and tourism sector are large; many pupils and families never reach a gate. Public remarks from Tourism and Wildlife leadership have called for digital platforms, student innovation, and technology that sends more income to communities. OFF TOUR fits that direction: a digital park window, classroom use, and an agreed share for the park and neighbours, recorded on **peaq** (identity, booking held until delivery, signed sightings without sensitive coordinates, split agreed before pricing). Today the visit is a simulation; real drives need Kenya Wildlife Service approval.

My prior work is on the record: **Maritime Anomaly System** (drone project) with a team of two at the **NIRU National AI Hackathon** (podium finish), presented at the **3rd Mashariki Cooperation Conference** in Diani (April 2026). OFF TOUR is the parks and education build that follows.

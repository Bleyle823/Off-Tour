# Product update — ground rovers only (no drones)

**Project:** OFF TOUR  
**From:** Bleyle Osewe · Bennybleylle09@gmail.com · 0705297628  
**Date:** 10 October 2026

---

**Summary for partners and programme organisers**

OFF TOUR has **moved away from any drone or aerial aspect**. The product is now **unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) only**: an autonomous **Clearpath Moose**–class rover on **designated park roads** (tourist self-drive circuits and ranger patrol routes on the same road network).

- **Remote visitors** steer the **camera**, not the wheels. The rover follows a **trained path** and keeps an agreed **viewing distance**.
- **Aircraft are not part of OFF TOUR** — no park overflights, no live aerial feeds in this product.
- The **simulation and demo** show the UGV on a dirt road in savannah terrain (Webots), not a drone.

**Separate earlier work:** At the NIRU National AI Hackathon I built **Maritime Anomaly System**, a **drone** project for maritime use. That work is **not** OFF TOUR. OFF TOUR is a **ground** conservation and tourism service for Kenya’s parks.

If any earlier materials mentioned drones in the same breath as OFF TOUR, please treat this note as the current scope: **UGV rovers on roads only.**

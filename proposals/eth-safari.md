Dear ETHSafari organisers,

I have been part of this community for some time — through the hackathons and the first ETHSafari. I am writing to ask you to **recognise OFF TOUR** as a Kenyan conservation and tourism use of Web3. I am not asking for programme space, a booth, or a demo slot. I am asking for **formal recognition** of the project as a real-world African use case aligned with what ETHSafari has supported: builders who show purpose, not only tokens.

**OFF TOUR** lets someone who is not in the park follow wildlife on a designated self-drive circuit, while rangers can use a separate patrol path for fence lines, snares, and dry-season fire risk. In simulation, an autonomous Clearpath Moose stays on the trained tourist route and keeps distance; the remote visitor controls the camera only. Rhinos are never on a public feed and their locations are not published. The product is aimed at Kenya’s parks in general, with a practical first step at **Nairobi National Park** if written approval follows. Other parks would follow only with each park’s consent. No approval has been granted yet. Aircraft are not part of the product.

Kenya’s tourism sector is large and increasingly decided on a phone. Public remarks from Tourism and Wildlife leadership have linked digital platforms, innovation, and tools such as blockchain to tourism growth and community income. OFF TOUR is my contribution from that direction — as a BBIT alumnus (Class of 2024, Africa Nazarene University): a digital visit with classroom use and a checkable share for the park and neighbouring communities.

The demo today is a simulation (savannah, road, herd, autonomous rover). **peaq** is the public record layer: machine identity for the rover, booking held until delivery, payment release on completion, signed sightings and patrol logs with sensitive coordinates withheld and people blurred. Mobile-money payment via a licensed provider (without a crypto wallet) is planned. Some elements are on testnet; some remain mocked in the simulation — a production mainnet step is still ahead.

My path to this build is on the record. With a team of two at the **NIRU National AI Hackathon**, I built **Maritime Anomaly System** (drone project) to a podium finish. In April 2026 we presented that work at the **3rd Mashariki Cooperation Conference** in Diani. OFF TOUR is the follow-on: parks, classrooms, and community share on peaq, with the rover on a trained path and permission from Kenya Wildlife Service still to be secured in writing.

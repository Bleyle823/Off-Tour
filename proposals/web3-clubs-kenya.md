Dear Web3 Clubs,

I am a member of Web3 Clubs and I come to the lab for sessions and talks. I am writing to ask you to **recognise OFF TOUR** as a member project that uses Web3 for a public service in Kenya. I am not asking for a community demo slot, mentorship meetings, or introductions. I am asking for **recognition** only.

**OFF TOUR** is a remote visit to a Kenyan park. In simulation, an autonomous Clearpath Moose stays on designated tourist self-drive paths and keeps distance from animals. It is trained on that circuit and drives the path by itself. There is no driver in the vehicle; the remote person turns the camera only. Rangers can use a separate road patrol for fence checks, snares, and early fire detection. Rhinos are never on a public feed and their locations are never published.

The service is for Kenya’s parks in general, starting with **Nairobi National Park** where a first classroom or remote session is practical. Other parks would follow only with written permission. Nothing has been approved yet. Aircraft are not part of this product.

Tourism is a major sector in Kenya; park fees and visitation are published each year. Cabinet Secretary Rebecca Miano has publicly linked digital platforms and blockchain to cutting leakage so more income reaches communities and conservancies. OFF TOUR uses **peaq** so a visitor, a park, and neighbours can check booking, delivery, and an agreed income split on a shared record — not only on private books. The visit is simulated today; identity, held booking, signed sightings (without sensitive coordinates), and an agreed split are the Web3 layer. Mobile money through a licensed provider is planned for schools and local visitors without a crypto wallet. Testnet and mocked parts will stay labelled until mainnet and real park permission.

I have taken **Maritime Anomaly System** to a podium finish at the **NIRU National AI Hackathon** and presented that drone work at the **3rd Mashariki Cooperation Conference** in Diani (April 2026). OFF TOUR is the build that follows.

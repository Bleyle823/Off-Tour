# Draft: letter to KWS (edit before sending)

**Subject:** Proposal for a road-based telepresent safari and ranger patrol — Nairobi National Park

Dear [Warden / Park Management],

We are writing to propose **OFF TOUR**, an autonomous Clearpath Moose that stays on
designated tourist self-drive paths. It is trained on that circuit and drives it by itself.
It is meant to support KWS rangers and to let people who cannot travel see the park,
without adding a driver, aircraft, or off-road driving.

**What we are not asking for:** drones, flights, driving off the road, chasing wildlife,
or any live public imagery of the rhino sanctuary. Remote guests would control a camera
only. The rover is trained on the designated tourist self-drive circuit and drives that
path by itself. There is no driver in the vehicle. The path geofence and viewing-distance
rules still outrank that autonomy, and written approval is still required before a real circuit.

**What we offer first (Ranger Patrol):**
- Supervised patrols on an agreed circuit: fence checks from the road, cues for snares or
  carcasses, and dry-season heat and smoke alerts from a mast sensor at legal stops.
- A published wildlife protocol: viewing distance, a speed cap near herds, and a stop or
  withdraw when animals show vigilance.
- Tamper-evident patrol logs and signed detection records shareable with KWS.

**What we would ask only later (Remote Seat):**
- Short, pre-booked school and public sessions on the same approved roads, after patrol
  use has earned trust.
- A defined KWS share of any session revenue (see attached split policy).

We would welcome a short meeting to align on circuits, times, and welfare monitoring
before any equipment enters the park.

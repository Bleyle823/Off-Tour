# Draft: letter to KWS (edit before sending)

**Subject:** Proposal for conservation-first drone monitoring partnership — Nairobi National Park

Dear [Warden / Park Management],

We are writing to propose **Off-Safari**, a conservation-first unmanned aircraft programme
designed to support KWS rangers—not replace them—with stand-off wildlife monitoring,
fence-line checks, and signed data for patrol planning.

**What we are not asking for initially:** recreational drone tourism, flights over visitor
roads, or any live public imagery of the rhino sanctuary.

**What we offer first (Ranger Eye):**
- Thermal and visual patrols at agreed altitudes and distances, with a published wildlife
  disturbance protocol based on peer-reviewed stand-off guidelines.
- Tamper-evident flight logs and signed detection records shareable with KWS.
- Revenue from any future public “remote seat” sessions would include a defined KWS share
  (see attached split policy).

**Regulatory:** Operations would be conducted only under KCAA authorisation, with a licensed
remote pilot in command, and with written KWS approval per your drone policy and fee schedule.

We would welcome a short meeting to align on zones, times, and welfare monitoring before any
equipment enters the park.

Respectfully,
